🚨 Key Planchet Flaws:

1. Cartoonish Gang Metallurgy & Third-Sequel Script Exhaust

  • By its third installment, Death Wish abandons the gritty moral weight of the 1974 original for a comic-book framework. Paul Kersey returns to a war-torn East New York to face theatrical, broad-daylight villains led by Fraker and his reverse-Mohawk, trading realistic police procedure for cartoonish escalation.

2. Uncalibrated Urban Warfare & Second-Act Logic Leaks

  • The film’s structural logic dissolves into exploitation fantasy as the neighborhood transforms into a full-scale combat zone with rocket launchers and booby traps. The NYPD essentially greenlights Kersey’s vigilante war, keeping the movie from climbing into higher grading registers.

3. High-Caliber Cannon Films Strike Power (The F-14 Anchor)

  • Despite its narrative flaws, the film earns an F-14 certification in the F-12 to F-15 Registry as an elite guilty pleasure. It delivers peak 1980s action driven by Charles Bronson’s deadpan performance, Jimmy Page’s guitar score, practical explosions, and an unforgettable climax featuring a Browning .30-caliber machine gun.

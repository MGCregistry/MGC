📜 THE FULL INQUEST BENCH ASSESSMENT & LUSTER LOG

  • Baseline Bench Assessment: Room Allocation ➔ MS-62 Choice Mint State The asset earns its high grade through Robert De Niro and Sharon Stone’s sharp performances, Robert Richardson’s vibrant, high-contrast cinematography, and a period-authentic soundtrack that propels the narrative. However, closer inspection reveals four structural flaws holding it at an MS-63 rating:

1. The Backyard Spy Plane

  • An FBI surveillance aircraft running out of fuel and landing on Ace Rothstein’s private golf green feels like a sudden Hollywood shortcut that interrupts the film’s grounded realism.

2. Over-Expositional Voiceover

  • Heavy, overlapping narration by Ace and Nicky occasionally forces the film to tell rather than show, creating a dense information dump in the first act.

3. Domestic Drag

  • The second act slows down significantly due to a repetitive cycle of explosive marital conflicts between Ace and Ginger.

4. Goodfellas Mirroring

  • The film shares a nearly identical dynamic and rise-and-fall structure with Scorsese’s earlier work Goodfellas, preventing it from feeling completely unique.

🏛 ⚖️ THE SUPREME OVERRULING & MS-65 FINAL ASSESSMENT

  • 1. The Backyard Spy Plane Miracle: SUSTAINED — The coincidental landing of a fuel-depleted federal surveillance aircraft on Ace’s golf green is sustained as a minor planchet blemish that momentarily interrupts the film’s true-crime realism.
  • 2. Over-Expositional Voiceover Cross-Fire: OVERRULED — The dual narration by Ace and Nicky is validated; far from a defect, this rhythmic storytelling style masterfully details how the mob controlled Vegas.
  • 3. The Exploding Mid-Section Domestic Drag: SUSTAINED — The repetitive cycles of domestic warfare between Ace and Ginger are sustained as a minor circulation drag that slows the narrative momentum before the final collapse.
  • 4. The Goodfellas Mirroring Pedigree: SUSTAINED (Barely) — While sharing structural DNA with Scorsese’s earlier work, the formula is executed with such elite mastery that it retains a Mint State grade.
